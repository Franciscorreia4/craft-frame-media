FAQ

Questions we get asked

What festival video do you produce?

Aftermovies, artist and DJ highlight reels, live multi-cam event coverage, same-day edits, social and Reels content, and backstage and artist content.

Do you film club nights as well as festivals?

Yes — we cover festivals and nightclubs.

Where are you based, and where do you work?

We're based in Warsaw and Lisbon and work worldwide.

How much does a video project cost?

Every project is quoted individually, based on the event, the coverage you need and the deliverables. Email us the date, the location and what the content is for, and we'll come back with a quote.

Can you deliver edits on the same day?

Yes — same-day edits are available when the event calls for it. For longer pieces such as aftermovies, we agree the timeline with you up front.

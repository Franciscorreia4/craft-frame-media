Aftermovie from Surround's show with Korolova & Camelphat at Sektor6D, Warsaw's brand-new electronic music venue — shot and edited in under 24 hours.

The job

Cover every artist on the night with multiple cameras, post content while the show was still running, and deliver the aftermovie fast.

What made it different

Sektor6D is a brand-new venue in Warsaw. We posted content live during the show while we kept filming, and the aftermovie was finished in less than 24 hours.

The result

Surround published the content on its channels, and its feedback was positive.

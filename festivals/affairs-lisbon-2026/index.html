Affairs Lisbon 2026: an aftermovie and highlights posted live

Affairs is Vintage Culture's own label and event brand. In Lisbon it drew 15,000 people, and the organiser hired Craft Frame Media to cover it.

Team
Small crew (2–3 people)

Aftermovie from Affairs Lisbon 2026 — Vintage Culture's own label and event brand, headlined by Vintage Culture himself in front of 15,000 people in Lisbon, Portugal, with Patrick Topping, Volkoder, Doozie, and Kevin Brauer also on the lineup.

The job

Two kinds of deliverable: the aftermovie for after the event, and highlight videos while it was still running.

What made it different

Besides the main aftermovie, we shot and edited three highlight videos during the event. They were shot, edited and posted while the event was still going on, so the content reached the audience as the night happened.

The result

The organiser's feedback was positive.

“The video is epic. Watching it gave me goosebumps”

— The Affairs organiser

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Craft Frame Media ("we", "us") produces video and photography for events and brands. This page explains what personal data this website collects and how it's handled.

Who runs this site

Craft Frame Media – Francisco Correia, registered in Poland.
VAT/Tax ID: PL5214166806

What this site does not do

This site runs no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies. It doesn't run a contact form, so it never collects your name, email, or message through the site itself.

What it does store

The only thing this site saves in your browser is your English/Polish language choice, kept in localStorage on your own device so the site remembers it next time you visit. It's never transmitted anywhere and isn't a cookie.

Third-party services this site loads

Loading this page requests resources from a few outside services, each of which sees your IP address and basic request metadata (standard for any web request) when it's called:

  • Netlify — hosts this site and serves every page you load here.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — serves the animation library (GSAP) used for scroll effects.

None of these are configured by us to track you across other sites. If you'd rather not make these requests at all, most browsers let you block third-party requests, though parts of the site (fonts, scroll animations) won't work as intended.

If you contact us

If you email hello@craftframe-media.com or message us on Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to send (your email address or Instagram handle, and your message) so we can reply. We use it only to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver the project — never sold or passed on to third parties.

Questions

Reach out to hello@craftframe-media.com with any question about this policy or about data we hold on you.
